## ESP32 + Rust

ESP32 is a low-cost Wi-Fi and Bluetooth IoT chip family from Espressif. This series covers Rust development on ESP32-C3/C6/S3.

### Supported chips
- ESP32-C3 (RISC-V)
- ESP32-C6 (RISC-V, Wi-Fi 6)
- ESP32-S3 (dual-core Xtensa)

### Development tools
- esp-hal
- Embassy
- espflash

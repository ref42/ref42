## STM32 + Rust

STM32 is a family of ARM Cortex-M microcontrollers from STMicroelectronics. This series covers Rust development on STM32Fxx and STM32Hxx devices.

### Supported chips
- STM32F4 family
- STM32H7 family

### Development tools
- Embassy async framework
- probe-rs
